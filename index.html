Help & support

Where are my letters stored?

On your iPhone and in your device backup. If you sign in with Apple, they also sync through your own private iCloud. We run no servers and can't see your letters.

How do I move my letters to a new phone?

Sign in with Apple on the new phone and your letters sync from your iCloud. Restoring from a backup works too. You can also export all recordings as a .zip from Settings → Keep & share.

How do I cancel Dear Little Plus?

Open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Dear Little. Your letters are never deleted when a subscription ends.
