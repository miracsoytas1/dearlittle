Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Dear Little ("the app") is built so that your letters to your child stay yours. The short version: we do not collect, store, or share any of your data.

What the app stores

Your child's name and birthday, your voice recordings, written letters, notes, photos you attach and your settings are stored on your device, inside the app's private storage, and, if you turn on sync, in your private iCloud. They are included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone if you have backups turned on. We have no servers and no access to this data.

Sign in with Apple and iCloud sync (optional)

You can use Dear Little without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous user identifier and, only the first time, the name and email you choose to share (you can hide your email). These are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

When you are signed in, your children's names, letters, recordings and photos are synced through your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We, the developers, cannot access it.

You can sign out at any time, or delete your account in Settings > Account > Delete account, which removes the iCloud copy from all your devices. To stop sharing your Apple ID with Dear Little entirely, go to iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Sign in with Apple.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording a voice letter. Recordings are saved on your device.

Speech recognition

If you use transcription (a Dear Little Plus feature), your recording is transcribed using Apple's Speech framework. Where supported, this happens entirely on your device. On devices or languages without on-device support, Apple may process the audio according to Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive the audio or the text.

Photos

When you add a photo, you choose it with the system photo picker. The app only receives the single photo you pick and saves a copy on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

Analytics, ads and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is intended for use by parents and guardians. It does not collect any personal information from children or anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual letters or a child's whole capsule in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device (copies may remain in your own backups).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
