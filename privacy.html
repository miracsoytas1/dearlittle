Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Dear Little ("the app") is built so that your letters to your child stay yours. The short version: we, the developers, never receive your letters, recordings, photos or account details. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which is explained below. Dear Little Plus has no ads.

What the app stores

Your child's name and birthday, your voice recordings, written letters, notes, photos you attach and your settings are stored on your device, inside the app's private storage, and, if you turn on sync, in your private iCloud. They are included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone if you have backups turned on. We have no servers and no access to this data.

Sign in with Apple and iCloud sync (optional)

You can use Dear Little without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous user identifier and, only the first time, the name and email you choose to share (you can hide your email). These are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

When you are signed in, your children's names, letters, recordings and photos are synced through your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We, the developers, cannot access it.

You can sign out at any time, or delete your account in Settings > Account > Delete account, which removes the iCloud copy from all your devices. To stop sharing your Apple ID with Dear Little entirely, go to iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Sign in with Apple.

Writing together (optional, Dear Little Plus)

If you invite the other parent, Apple's iCloud sharing makes your child's capsule visible to the people you invite. Each letter and photo shows the name you entered under "They call you". You choose who is invited and can remove people or stop sharing at any time. Shared data stays in iCloud under Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot access it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording a voice letter. Recordings are saved on your device.

Speech recognition

If you use transcription (a Dear Little Plus feature), your recording is transcribed using Apple's Speech framework. Where supported, this happens entirely on your device. On devices or languages without on-device support, Apple may process the audio according to Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive the audio or the text.

Photos and camera

When you add a photo, you choose it with the system photo picker or take it with the camera. The app only receives the single photo you pick or take and saves a copy on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on).

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. Dear Little Plus has no ads, and the ad SDK is not started for Plus users. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information and ad interactions. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. iOS also asks whether the app may track you; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier.

Ads never have access to your letters, recordings, photos or your child's information. The app contains no analytics SDK.

Children

The app is intended for use by parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the content of the app (your letters) is never shared with advertisers.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual letters or a child's whole capsule in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device (copies may remain in your own backups).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
